Multi-currency ledger

NimbusLedger posts in 42 currencies. Each entity has one functional currency, set at entity creation and immutable.

FX rates

Daily rates are sourced from the ECB 16:00 CET fix. Weekends and TARGET holidays reuse the last published rate. You may override a rate on a journal, which flags the line as manual-fx in audit.

We do not ingest Bloomberg or Reuters mid-rates. Crypto-assets are not currencies in the ledger; record them as inventory accounts.

Translation

At period lock, monetary balances are remeasured at the period-end rate. CTA (cumulative translation adjustment) posts to equity account 3900 unless remapped.

Rounding

Amounts are stored at 6 decimal places internally and rounded to currency minor units on reports. A residual under 0.01 in functional currency is posted to 6950 FX rounding.
