# Support SLAs

Severity is set by the customer on ticket open and may be downgraded by NimbusLedger after triage.

| Severity | Meaning | First response | Update cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Production posting or login fully down | **30 minutes** | every 30 minutes |
| P2 | Major feature impaired, workaround exists | **4 hours** | every 4 hours |
| P3 | Minor defect or how-to | **next business day** | at resolution or 3 business days, whichever first |

Business hours for P3 are 09:00–18:00 in the workspace home region (US Eastern or CET). P1 and P2 are 24×7 for Growth and Scale. Starter P1 is business hours only.

Premium support (add-on) shortens P1 first response to **15 minutes** and assigns a named CSM.

Status page: https://status.nimbusledger.com. Credits: 10% of monthly subscription for any P1 lasting more than 4 hours, capped at 50% of that month.
