# NimbusLedger Pricing (FY2026)

NimbusLedger is a multi-entity general ledger for mid-market finance teams.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Included legal entities | Journal lines / month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $49 | 3 | 25,000 |
| Growth | $199 | 25 | 250,000 |
| Scale | $799 | unlimited | 2,000,000 |

Annual prepay is 10 months for the price of 12 (two months free).

## Add-ons

- Extra legal entity on Starter or Growth: $12 / entity / month.
- Close copilot (period-end checklist + flux comments): $80 / month per workspace.
- Premium support (named CSM + P1 15-minute ack): $400 / month. Not available on Starter.

## Overage

Journal-line overage is billed at $0.40 per 1,000 lines on Starter and Growth. Scale has no line overage; instead, API throughput above the Scale cap is queued, not billed.

## Seat model

Pricing is **entity-based, not seat-based**. Unlimited controller, accountant, and read-only seats are included on every plan. External auditor seats are included and expire 90 days after invite.

## Taxes

US customers are billed in USD. EU customers may elect EUR. Prices exclude VAT. NimbusLedger supports OSS for EU VAT on subscriptions; MOSS is **not** supported.
